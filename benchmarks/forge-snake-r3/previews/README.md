# Iteration previews — NOT judged captures

All `PREVIEW-*` images are low-sample Cycles previews (32 samples for assembly/head;
48 samples for detail views). Judged 256-sample captures live in `../captures/`.

- **a:** first actual-eye checkpoint. Skull length/height and tiny recessed optics
  improved, but finish read too clean and hardware layering was too subtle.
- **d:** raised/notched lap tiles, added physical notched crown and cooling
  crosswires, smaller crest. This check exposed faceting caused by a trial optical
  decimation; that trial was rejected.
- **f:** socket/liner checkpoint. Smooth original optical tessellation restored;
  1,000 triangles reclaimed from the concealed skull liner instead. Finite drag
  scratches and edge-confined directional abrasion strengthened. No broad rust
  noise field was restored. This close-up revealed comb-like abrasion, corrected
  in **g** by lowering its frequency and making edge wear sparser.
- **g:** build-only weather revision, irregular edge chips/scuffs, finite scratches and
  anchored runoff; no regular comb-like bands.
- **h:** final checkpoint, darkened and physically beveled crown hardware.
- **detail-module / detail-tail:** final checkpoint at closer range, showing
  geometric plate sidewalls, fasteners, crosswires, piston hardware and fin guards.

The R2 target and both original judged R2 images were viewed in this session
before the first build, and these checkpoints were viewed before production.
